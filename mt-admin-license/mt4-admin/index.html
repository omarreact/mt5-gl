MT4 Admin License for Brokers Who Want Full Control of Their Platform

MT4 is still the platform a huge number of forex traders reach for first. It’s familiar, dependable and backed by years of custom indicators and expert…

Talk to MetaGreyLabel ↗Explore Grey Label

MT4 is still the platform a huge number of forex traders reach for first. It’s familiar, dependable and backed by years of custom indicators and expert advisors. For a broker whose clients live on MT4, owning the server behind it can be a serious advantage.

An MT4 admin license puts that server in your hands. Your company holds the licence, your team has administrator access and every decision about how the platform runs is yours. This page covers what that involves, what it costs, how setup works and what you should know before you commit.

01

What an MT4 Admin License Gives You

With your own licence, you’re no longer operating inside someone else’s environment. The MT4 server, its configuration and everything running on it belong to your brokerage.

Administrator-Level Access

You control server settings, plugins, gateways, data feeds, backups and security. Your team can make changes whenever needed without asking a provider for permission.

Full Control Over Trading Operations

Groups, symbols, spreads, swaps, leverage, trading sessions and execution settings are all set by you. You can build your offer exactly the way your clients need it.

Ownership of Client Data

Trading history, client records and server logs sit on infrastructure your company controls. That makes compliance reviews and audits far more straightforward.

02

An Important Note on MT4 Server License Availability

Before you plan around an MT4 server license, there’s something you should know. MetaQuotes has focused heavily on MT5 for several years and has largely stopped issuing brand new MT4 server licences.

That doesn’t always rule MT4 out. Some brokers already hold MT4 licences, and in certain cases there may be other routes worth exploring. But it does mean availability needs checking early, before you build your plans around it.

Why We Mention This Upfront

Some providers skip over this until well into the process. We’d rather you know at the start, so you can weigh MT4 against MT5 properly and avoid wasting time or money.

The MT5 Alternative

If a new MT4 licence isn’t available for your situation, an MT5 admin licence is usually the natural option. MT5 supports forex alongside metals, indices, stocks and futures, and most MT4 traders adapt to it within a few days.

03

Understanding MT4 Admin Cost

The MT4 admin cost is made up of several pieces, and it’s worth looking at each one rather than a single headline figure.

Licence Fees

Where an MT4 licence is available, the licence fee itself is set by MetaQuotes or determined by the specific arrangement. This is usually the largest upfront cost.

Hosting and Infrastructure

Your server needs to run in a reliable data centre, ideally close to your liquidity providers for low latency. Hosting costs depend on location, performance requirements and backup needs.

Integrations

Liquidity bridge, forex CRM, client portal, payment gateways and risk management plugins all add to the total. Some brokers already have several of these in place.

Ongoing Management

Someone has to keep the server healthy. That could be your own team, a managed service from us, or a combination. Monthly costs depend on how much support you need.

Getting an Accurate Figure

Because so much depends on your setup, the most reliable way to get a real number is to share your plans with us. We’ll send back an itemised estimate showing exactly where the money goes.

04

MT4 Admin Setup: How the Process Works

A good MT4 admin setup is mostly about careful planning. Rushing it is how brokers end up with downtime and unhappy clients.

Step One: Assessment

We look at your current position. Do you already hold a licence? Are you moving from a grey label? Which markets are you serving? That shapes the whole plan.

Step Two: Infrastructure

We help you choose hosting, set up the server environment and configure it for performance, stability and security.

Step Three: Configuration

Groups, symbols, trading conditions, plugins and gateways are set up to match your business model. If you’re moving from a grey label, we replicate your existing setup and improve on it where needed.

Step Four: Integrations and Testing

Your CRM, client portal, payments and liquidity bridge are connected and tested together. Your team runs through real scenarios before anything goes live.

Step Five: Launch and Support

You go live with full administrator access. We stay involved for as long as you want, from full server management to occasional specialist help.

05

Who an MT4 Admin License Suits

Owning an MT4 server makes most sense for established brokers with strong MT4 audiences, firms already holding an MT4 licence who need better setup and management, and grey label brokers whose volumes now justify their own infrastructure. You’ll also want either a technical team or a reliable partner to manage the server day to day.

If you’re new to the industry, a grey label is often the better starting point. We’ll tell you honestly which fits.

06

Take Full Control of Your MT4 Brokerage

An MT4 admin license gives you ownership, control and credibility that shared infrastructure can’t match. But it’s a big step, and it deserves honest advice and careful planning.

07

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I still get a new MT4 admin licence?

New MT4 server licences are very limited, as MetaQuotes now focuses on MT5. Availability depends on your situation, so it’s best to check early. If MT4 isn’t possible, we’ll walk you through MT5 as an alternative.

I already have an MT4 licence. Can you manage it for me?

Yes. We can take over setup, hosting, configuration and ongoing management of an existing MT4 server, or support your in-house team where they need help.

Can I run MT5 alongside my MT4 server?

Yes. Many brokers keep MT4 for existing clients and add MT5 for new instruments and newer traders. We can set up and manage both.

How long does MT4 admin setup take?

If you already hold a licence, server setup and integrations can often be completed within a few weeks. Migrations from a grey label are usually done over a weekend to avoid disrupting traders.
