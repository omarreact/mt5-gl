03

What Affects Your Forex Broker Package Price

Your final forex broker package price depends on a few things beyond the tier you pick.

Platform Choice

Running MT4 or MT5 alone costs less than running both. If you only need one right now, you can add the other later.

Number of Integrations

Every extra payment gateway, liquidity provider or third-party tool adds configuration and testing work.

Customisation

Template websites and standard CRM workflows keep costs down. Fully custom designs and bespoke processes take more time to build.

Expected Client Volume

Larger client bases need more server resources and more support capacity, which is reflected in the monthly fee.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upgrade from the starter package later?

Yes. Many clients start on the starter package and move to Growth within their first year. We move your clients and data across with minimal disruption.

Are there any fees not shown in the package?

Routine changes and standard support are covered in your monthly fee. Anything outside your package, like a new integration, is quoted in advance so you can decide before any work begins.

How long does each package take to set up?

The starter package can usually go live within a few days once your branding and documents are ready. Growth and Professional packages take a little longer because of extra integrations and testing.

Can I pay the setup fee in stages?

In some cases, yes. Talk to us about your budget and we’ll see what we can arrange.
