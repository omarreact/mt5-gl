MT4 White Label for Brokers Who Want a Proven Platform From Day One

Some platforms come and go. MetaTrader 4 has stuck around for close to two decades, and there’s a simple reason. Traders know it, trust it and have built their whole routine around it. Their indicators, their expert advisors, their chart layouts, all of it lives on MT4.

If your target traders are already on MT4, giving them the same platform under your brand makes onboarding a lot easier. That’s exactly what an MT4 white label does. You get a fully working platform with your name on it, without buying a server licence or hiring a technical team.

01

What an MT4 White Label Actually Gives You

In plain terms, you’re operating on an existing MT4 server that’s already licensed, hosted and maintained. We brand the terminal for you, set up your trading groups and connect it to a back office, so you can start accepting clients without building the infrastructure yourself.

Your traders download a platform carrying your company name. They log in, fund their accounts and trade. They never need to know what’s running underneath.

Your Brand, Not Ours

Your logo, company name and colours appear across the terminal, the client portal and the welcome emails. For a new broker, looking established from the first login matters more than people think.

Familiar Ground for Traders

Because MT4 is so widely used, most traders need zero training. They can bring their custom indicators and EAs straight over. That removes one of the biggest reasons people hesitate before switching brokers.

02

Who Should Become a White Label MT4 Forex Broker?

Running as a white label MT4 forex broker suits a few types of businesses particularly well.

Introducing brokers who already bring in steady volume and want to keep more of the revenue. Trading educators and signal providers with a loyal community who’d rather trade with them directly. Financial firms adding forex alongside an existing product. And first-time founders who want to test their model in a real market before committing to anything bigger.

What these groups have in common is that they know how to find traders. What they usually don’t want is the cost and stress of running a server.

03

Why MT4 Still Makes Sense in 2026

MT5 gets most of the attention these days, and for good reason. But MT4 hasn’t gone anywhere. Huge numbers of forex traders still prefer it, particularly in markets where it’s been the standard for years.

A Massive Library of Tools

Thousands of indicators, scripts and expert advisors have been written for MT4. Many of them have no MT5 version. Traders who rely on them will choose a broker that supports MT4 every time.

Simple and Stable

MT4 is lighter and simpler than its successor. For pure forex trading it does the job well, and experienced traders like that they know exactly how it behaves.

Run It Alongside MT5 If You Like

You don’t have to pick one forever. Plenty of brokers launch with MT4 and add MT5 later as they expand into indices, commodities or stocks. We can set up both whenever you’re ready.

04

MT4 White Label Turnkey: Everything in One Package

A trading platform on its own isn’t a brokerage. You still need a way to sign clients up, verify them, take deposits and pay out withdrawals. That’s where an MT4 white label turnkey package comes in.

Instead of sourcing each part from a different supplier, you get the whole setup from us, already connected and tested.

What the Turnkey Package Covers

Depending on your needs, the package can include a branded MT4 terminal for desktop, web and mobile, a forex CRM with client onboarding and KYC, a trader’s room where clients manage deposits and accounts, payment gateway connections, an IB module for partners and commissions, and a branded website to bring it all together.

Why One Supplier Makes Life Easier

When your CRM, payments and platform come from different companies, small problems bounce between them for days. Nobody owns the fix. With a turnkey setup, there’s one team responsible for making it all work, and one number to call when something doesn’t.

05

Understanding MT4 White Label Cost

This is usually the first question we get, and it’s a fair one. The honest answer is that MT4 white label cost depends on what you need. A bare platform costs far less than a full turnkey brokerage, and the gap between the two is significant.

What Affects the Price

A few things move the number up or down. The number of client accounts you expect. Whether you want MT4 only or MT4 and MT5 together. How much of the turnkey package you take, such as CRM, website and payment integrations. Any extra plugins, like copy trading or PAMM accounts. And the level of ongoing support you need.

Setup Fees Versus Monthly Fees

Most white label arrangements have two parts. A one-time setup fee that covers configuration, branding and integration, and a monthly fee for server access, maintenance and support. We break both down clearly in our proposal, so you know what you’re paying for and why.

No Surprises Later

Hidden charges are a common complaint in this industry. Extra fees for reports, plugin access or support hours that weren’t mentioned upfront. We list everything before you sign. If something changes, you’ll hear about it before it shows up on an invoice.
