Frequently asked questions

Common launch questions, answered clearly.

MT4 or MT5, which should I choose?

MT5 is newer, supports more asset classes and is where most new brokers are heading. MT4 still has a loyal trader base. Many brokers offer both, and either or both can be configured.

Can I switch to a different model later?

Yes. Moving from white label to grey label, or from grey label to a broader turnkey setup, can be planned as the brokerage grows.
