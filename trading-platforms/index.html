Forex Trading Platforms for Brokers That Traders Already Know and Trust

Your trading platform is where clients spend most of their time with you. It’s where they watch charts, place orders, test strategies and decide whether your brokerage feels reliable. If the platform lets them down, nothing else you offer will make up for it.

That’s why choosing the right forex trading platforms for brokers matters so much. We focus on MetaTrader, the platforms most traders already use and expect, and we give you every way to access them: desktop, web and mobile, all under your brand.

01

Why MetaTrader Is the Platform Traders Expect

There are plenty of trading platforms out there. MetaTrader remains the one most retail forex and CFD traders look for when choosing a broker.

Familiarity

Most traders have used MT4 or MT5 at some point. Offering it means there’s almost no learning curve. They can start trading the same day they fund their account.

A Huge Ecosystem

Thousands of indicators, expert advisors, scripts and signals are built for MetaTrader. Traders can bring their favourite tools with them, which is a big reason they choose one broker over another.

Proven Reliability

MetaTrader has been tested by millions of traders through every kind of market condition. Brokers and clients both know what to expect from it.

02

MT4 and MT5: Choosing Your Platform

We support both MetaTrader platforms, and many brokers offer the two side by side.

MT4

Built mainly for forex, MT4 is still loved by traders who rely on its huge library of indicators and EAs. It’s simple, stable and a strong choice if your audience is forex focused.

MT5

The newer platform supports forex alongside metals, energies, indices, stocks, futures and crypto CFDs. It offers more timeframes, more order types, market depth, an economic calendar and a much faster strategy tester. Most new brokers now lead with MT5.

Offering Both

Running both platforms lets every trader choose what they’re comfortable with. It widens your audience and removes one more reason for someone to look elsewhere.

03

MT4 MT5 Web Mobile Desktop: Trade Anywhere

Modern traders switch devices constantly. They analyse charts on a desktop at home, check positions on their phone during the day and sometimes log in from a browser on someone else’s computer. Offering MT4 MT5 web mobile desktop access means they can trade wherever they are.

Desktop Terminal

The full-featured version for serious analysis and automated trading. Advanced charting, custom indicators, expert advisors, multiple chart layouts and the strategy tester all live here.

WebTrader

Trade straight from a browser with no download needed. Ideal for clients who use shared or work computers, or who want quick access without installing software.

Mobile Apps for iOS and Android

Monitor positions, open and close trades, view charts and receive alerts on the go. For many clients, the mobile app is how they check in most often.

Synced Across Devices

Clients log in with the same account details on every device. Positions, balances and history stay consistent wherever they trade.

04

Your Brand on Every Platform

Your broker trading platform should look and feel like part of your business, not someone else’s.

Branded Terminals

Your logo, company name and server name appear across the platform, so clients always know who they’re trading with.

Branded Downloads and Links

Clients download your platform from your website and access WebTrader through your domain.

Consistent Experience

From the website to the trader’s room to the platform itself, clients move through your brand without confusing jumps between different looks and names.

05

How the Platform Connects to Everything Else

A trading platform doesn’t work in isolation. It sits at the centre of your brokerage, linked to every other system.

Your CRM opens accounts and syncs client data. Your trader’s room handles deposits, withdrawals and account management. Your liquidity bridge delivers prices and routes orders. Your back office and reporting tools pull trading data for finance, compliance and management. Your IB module calculates commissions from real trading activity.

With our setups, these connections are built and tested before you launch, so everything works together from the first trade.

06

Choosing How to Run Your Platform

How much control you have over your platform depends on the model you choose.

White Label

A branded platform on an existing server with limited control. Affordable and quick to launch.

Grey Label

Your own groups, trading conditions and manager access on an existing server. More independence without the cost of a full licence.

Admin Licence

Your own MetaTrader server, licensed in your name, with full administrator control.

We’ll help you choose the right option for your stage and budget, and you can move up as your brokerage grows.

07

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I offer both MT4 and MT5 to my clients?

Yes. Many brokers run both platforms, and clients can open accounts on either one through the same trader’s room.

Do clients need to download anything to trade?

Not necessarily. They can use WebTrader directly in their browser, or download the desktop terminal or mobile app if they prefer.

Will the platform carry my branding?

Yes. Your company name and logo appear on the platform, and clients access it through your website and domain.

Can clients use their own indicators and expert advisors?

Yes. MetaTrader supports custom indicators and EAs on the desktop terminal. MT4 tools need to be built for MT4, and MT5 tools for MT5.
