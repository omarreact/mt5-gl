Common brokerage questions

When does a grey label model make sense?

When a brokerage needs more control over groups, spreads and trading conditions without immediately taking on full administrator ownership.

Why is bridge technology important?

The bridge connects platform pricing and orders to liquidity and supports the intended execution model.

What belongs in the operational layer?

CRM, trader portal, back office, KYC, deposits, withdrawals and partner tools surround the trading platform.

Does this static website run a brokerage backend?

No. It presents the available infrastructure. Live platform, regulatory and operational services require a separate implementation agreement.
