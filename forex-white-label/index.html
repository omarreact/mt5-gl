Not every new brokerage needs its own server on day one. In fact, most don’t. If you’re testing a market, building a client base or simply want to keep your early costs down, a forex white label is often the smartest way to begin.

You get a working trading platform under your own brand, connected and ready for clients, without spending a fortune on licences and infrastructure. We handle the technical side. You focus on bringing in traders.

01

What Is a Forex White Label?

Think of it as renting a fully furnished office instead of building one from the ground up. The building, the wiring and the security already exist. You move in, put your name on the door and start working.

With a white label, your brokerage runs on an existing MetaTrader server that’s already licensed and maintained. Your clients log in to a platform carrying your brand, your logo and your company name. Behind the scenes, the heavy lifting is handled for you.

How It Works in Practice

We set up a branded MT4 or MT5 terminal for your clients, create your trading groups and connect everything to a back office you can manage. You decide how you market yourself, which clients you go after and how you build relationships with them. The server, updates and core maintenance stay with us.

Who It’s Built For

A white label works especially well for introducing brokers ready to move beyond earning rebates, first-time founders who want to prove their model before investing heavily, and established businesses adding forex to an existing financial offering. If you want to get trading quickly without a large upfront commitment, it’s a natural place to start.

02

Why Start as a White Label Forex Broker?

Starting as a white label forex broker gives you something every new business needs: room to learn without betting everything on day one.

Much Lower Upfront Costs

A full MetaTrader server licence is a serious investment, and that’s before hosting, integrations and staff. A white label strips most of that away. Your money can go into marketing, compliance and building a team instead.

Faster Time to Market

Because the infrastructure already exists, setup is quick. Once your paperwork and branding are ready, you can be welcoming clients far sooner than if you were building your own stack.

Less Technical Burden

You don’t need in-house server engineers or a dedicated IT team. Updates, stability and maintenance sit with us, which leaves you free to run the business side.

03

MT4 MT5 White Label: Which Platform Should You Offer?

Both platforms have loyal followings, and our MT4 MT5 white label options let you offer either one, or both side by side.

MT4 White Label

MT4 is still hugely popular, especially with forex traders who’ve used it for years and rely on their favourite indicators and expert advisors. If your target audience leans experienced and forex focused, MT4 can make onboarding much easier.

MT5 White Label

MT5 is the newer platform and supports a wider range of instruments, including stocks, indices, commodities and futures. It also offers more timeframes, better order handling and stronger testing tools. Most new brokers now lean towards MT5, particularly if they plan to offer more than currency pairs.

Offering Both

Plenty of brokers run both platforms so traders can choose what they’re comfortable with. It widens your audience and removes one more reason for a prospective client to look elsewhere.

04

What’s Included in Your White Label Setup

A platform alone doesn’t make a brokerage. Clients need to sign up, verify their identity, fund their accounts and get help when something goes wrong. Depending on your package, your setup can include a branded trading platform on MT4, MT5 or both, a forex CRM with KYC checks and a client portal, payment gateway integrations for cards, bank transfers and crypto, an IB module for managing partners and commissions, and a branded website and trader’s room.

Each piece is connected and tested before you go live, so data flows properly from the moment your first client signs up.

05

MetaTrader Grey Label White Label: Knowing the Difference

People often mix these two up, and it’s worth getting clear on before you commit. Our MetaTrader grey label white label options sit on the same infrastructure, but they give you different levels of control.

White Label

Lower cost, quicker setup and minimal technical responsibility. The trade-off is less control over things like spreads, risk settings and some group configurations, which remain under the main server’s oversight.

Grey Label

More independence. You get manager access, your own groups and much greater say over trading conditions. It costs more than a white label but far less than a full licence.

Moving Up When You’re Ready

Many clients start on a white label and upgrade to a grey label once their volumes and team grow. Because both run within our setup, the move is straightforward and your clients won’t notice any disruption. The flexibility of a MetaTrader grey label white label path means you never have to rebuild from scratch.
