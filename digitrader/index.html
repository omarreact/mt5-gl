A Forex Technology Provider That Gets You Live Fast

Let’s be honest about the options. You can build the whole stack yourself, and some firms do. But a full MT5 server licence is a serious expense, and the setup can drag on for the better part of a year once hosting, integrations and testing are in the mix.

A grey label sits in between. You run your own brand on a MetaTrader server that’s already set up and maintained, with your own manager access, your own groups and your own spreads. You aren’t borrowing someone else’s environment the way you would with a basic white label, and you aren’t carrying the full cost of a primary licence either.

As a forex technology provider, our job is to make that middle path simple. We look after the server side. You run the business.

What Grey Label Actually Means for You

You get an MT4 or MT5 environment under your company name, plus manager and admin terminals so your dealing team stays in control. Trading conditions, symbols, leverage and client groups are yours to set. And the upfront cost is a fraction of what a full licence would ask.

Who It Suits

New brokers testing a market. Introducing brokers who’ve outgrown rebates and want their own book. Established firms opening a second brand in a new region. If you want real control but don’t want to spend a year building, it’s probably a good fit.

MT4 or MT5, Which Should I Choose?

MT5 is newer, supports more asset classes and is where most new brokers are heading. MT4 still has a loyal trader base. Many brokers offer both, and we can set up either or both.

Can I Switch to a Different Model Later?

Yes. Moving from white label to grey label, or from grey label to a full forex white label grey label turnkey package, is something we handle regularly.

Forex White Label, Grey Label or Turnkey: Picking the Right Model

Not every broker needs the same thing. We’d rather you pick the right model than the most expensive one.

Brokerage
models

White Label

The lightest option. You operate under a larger broker’s server with your branding on top. It’s affordable and quick, but you get limited say over spreads, groups and risk settings. Good for testing the waters.

Grey Label

More control and more independence. You get your own manager access and can shape trading conditions the way you want. This is where most growing brokers land, and it’s our core offer.

Turnkey

Everything in one package. Platform, CRM, client portal, liquidity connections, payment integrations, website, the lot. If you’re starting from zero and want a single partner, a forex white label grey label turnkey setup saves you from juggling five vendors who all blame each other when something breaks.
